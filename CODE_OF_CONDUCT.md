# Code of conduct

Everyone participating in this project should be treated with respect.
Discuss engineering claims with evidence; welcome questions and corrections.
Respect privacy, differing experience and good-faith disagreement.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing and
publishing private information without permission are unacceptable. This applies
to project issues, pull requests and other spaces where someone represents the
project.

Maintainer: Suyog Joshi (GitHub `suyog19`). Report incidents privately through
the contact channel listed on the maintainer's [public profile](https://github.com/suyog19).
Do not disclose sensitive incident details in a public issue. If no suitable
private contact is available, request a private contact method without naming
affected people or describing the incident publicly.

The maintainer will consider reports, respect confidentiality as far as practical,
and may remove content, warn participants, restrict participation or ban repeated
or severe misconduct. Reporters and affected participants should have a chance
to provide context. No response-time guarantee is offered.
