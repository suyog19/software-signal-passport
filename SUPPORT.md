# Support

Use [GitHub issues](https://github.com/suyog19/software-signal-passport/issues)
for unclear fields, broken links, installation questions and sanitized trial
feedback. Include the template version, expected behavior and a small example.

Maintainer: Suyog Joshi (`suyog19`). This is a free, early artifact with
best-effort support and no response-time commitment. Support does not include
private repository audits, domain-policy decisions or consulting. Do not post
confidential material. See [security reporting](SECURITY.md) for vulnerabilities.

For conceptual questions, the
[canonical Passport page](https://github.com/suyog19/software-signal/blob/main/artifacts/ai-change-passport.md)
remains the source of truth. Product defects and distribution feedback belong here.
